Book Pantry

Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

Book Pantry is a personal book library. This page explains what the app keeps about you, who can see it and how to delete it. In short: we keep what the app needs to work, we don't track you, we don't show ads and we don't sell anything about you.

What we keep

  • Your account. When you sign in with Apple, Apple gives us an account ID, and your name and email address if you choose to share them. If you hide your email, we get a private relay address from Apple instead.
  • Your library. The books you add, whether you own them, your reading status and dates, your ratings, your reviews and notes, and your wishlist.
  • Sharing. Who you share your library with, and the invites you send.
  • Searches. When you search for a book or scan a barcode, our server looks it up and logs the lookup with your account ID, so we can keep usage fair and spot problems.
  • Shelf scans. When you scan a shelf, the camera, the outlines and the reading of titles and authors all happen on your iPhone. The pictures never leave it and are deleted when you finish reviewing the shelf. Only the text read from each book is sent to our server, to look it up like a typed search. Books we can't match straight away are kept in a queue with your account ID while we look them up, and deleted once they're a day old.

Where it's kept

Your account and library are stored with Supabase, the service that runs our database and server. A copy of your library is also kept on your iPhone so the app opens instantly and works offline.

Performance reports from Apple (MetricKit) are saved on your iPhone only. They are not sent to us.

Who can see it

  • You.
  • People you share with. They can see your library and wishlist, including ownership, reading status and ratings. They can't see your reviews or notes. You can stop sharing at any time in the app.
  • Nobody else. We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Book details and covers

Book details come from Open Library. Our server asks Open Library for them, so your searches reach Open Library from our server, not from your phone. Cover images are loaded by your phone directly from Open Library, so Open Library sees your phone's internet address when it shows a cover, as any website would. Open Library doesn't learn who you are from this.

No tracking

The app has no ads, no analytics services and no tracking across other apps or websites.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Account and tap Delete Account. Your account, library, ratings, reviews and sharing are deleted straight away and can't be recovered. Our search logs keep the count of lookups but no longer point to you. Signing out removes the copy on your iPhone.

Children

Book Pantry isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
