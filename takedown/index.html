Book Pantry

Copyright and Takedowns

Book Pantry shows book covers from Open Library, linked by Open Library's cover ID. We don't copy or host the images ourselves.

Asking us to remove a cover

If you hold the rights to a cover and don't want Book Pantry to show it, email lee.higgitt@gmail.com with:

  • the book's ISBN, or its title and author,
  • who you are and your connection to the rights holder,
  • a way to contact you.

We'll stop showing the cover within 7 days and reply to let you know. The app shows a plain placeholder in its place.

Removing it from Open Library

We can stop showing an image, but we can't remove it from Open Library. To have it removed there too, contact Open Library.
