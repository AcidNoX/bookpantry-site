Support

Something not working, or an idea for the app? Email lee.higgitt@gmail.com. Tell us what you were doing and what you expected, and include a screenshot if you can.

Common questions

A book has the wrong details or no cover. Book details and covers come from Open Library. You can fix the details there, and we pick up the change the next time we refresh that book. Email us if it's still wrong.

How do I share my library? Open Account, tap Sharing and send an invite. The other person sees your library and wishlist, but not your reviews or notes. You can stop sharing from the same screen.

How do I delete my account? Open Account and tap Delete Account. Everything is deleted straight away. See the Privacy Policy for details.
